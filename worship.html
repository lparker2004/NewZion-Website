Online Worshippers

You Are Not Bolted On, You Are Integrated

Many churches stream their services. We do something different, we treat our online worshippers as members of the congregation, not observers of it. You are part of the service. We see you. We greet you. We worship with you.

During our Sunday meet & greet, in-person members open the YouTube chat on their phones and greet online worshippers by name. This is one congregation, two locations.

1
Tune In

Click Watch Live above at 11:15 AM Sunday. The stream starts a few minutes early.

2
Say Hello

During meet & greet, drop your name and city in the YouTube chat. We will read you out loud.

3
Worship With Us

Open your Bible. Participate in the service. You are not watching, you are worshipping.

Teaching & Equipping

Go Deeper in the Word Every Week

Wednesday Bible Study is one of the most important hours of the week at New Zion. It is where believers move from knowing about Scripture to understanding it, and from understanding it to living it.

Every session is streamed live and archived on our YouTube channel, so you never have to miss a teaching even if life gets in the way.

No registration required. Just show up, open your Bible, and bring your questions. All are welcome.
