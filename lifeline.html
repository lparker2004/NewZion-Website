Why This Page Exists

One Person. One Act of Care.

A man had decided to end his life. Before he did, he made himself one final promise — if he could find just one person who cared whether he lived or died, he would not go through with it.

Someone reached out.

He is alive today. And now he spends his life turning others away from the same edge he once stood on. That one act of human kindness — that one moment of someone saying “I see you, and you matter” — changed everything.

We believe in that kind of love here. This page exists because of it.

We are glad you found this page. Please reach out.

Reach Out

Send a Private Message

The form to the right goes directly to our pastoral team. You do not need to share your name or email — though if you'd like a personal response, leaving an email address makes that possible.

There is no wrong way to use this form. Write as much or as little as you need to. We will receive it, read it, and respond.

Immediate Crisis Support

If You Need Help Right Now

If you are in immediate danger, call 911. If you need someone to talk to right now — please do not wait for a response from this form. The 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and is completely free and confidential.

After You Submit

What Happens Next

We want you to know exactly what to expect after you send your message — no mystery, no silence.

Step 1

Your Message Is Received

The moment you submit, your message is securely delivered to our pastoral team. You will see a confirmation on screen.

Step 2

A Real Person Reads It

Rev. Parker reads every message personally. Your words are not going into a void — they land with someone who cares.

Step 3

We Respond Within 24 Hours

If you left an email address, expect a personal response within 24 hours. If you remained anonymous, we will be praying for you.
