Enroll New Member

Use this right when someone tells you they want to join. Enter their name, phone, email, and how they are joining. That is all. A welcome email goes to them automatically with a link to finish their full profile, so no one falls through the cracks.

How it works

  1. You enter the four details below.
  2. They receive a welcome email with a link to complete their full profile.
  3. New Believers and anyone requesting Re-Baptism are flagged so Pastor can schedule baptism.
  4. Everyone except Casual Members is invited to the New Member Orientation.

Enrolling someone in person? You can open the New Member Welcome page with them.
