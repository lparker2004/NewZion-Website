About Us

A Historic Church. A Living Congregation. A New Generation.

New Zion Baptist Church is a small, historic congregation in Washington, D.C. with a mission far larger than our building.

Who We Are

Rooted Locally. Reaching the World.

New Zion has served Washington, D.C. since 1983 as a teaching-centered congregation called to equip believers through the clear, faithful explanation of God's Word. We are intentionally a small church with a large mission, and we've come to see that intimacy as a strength, not something to outgrow.

Our physical congregation gathers at 5400 Call Place SE, but our calling extends well beyond our walls. Through online worship, Bible study, and a growing library of teaching, we're becoming an accessible source of Christ-centered training for believers anywhere in the world.

Our Mission

Equipping Believers Through the Clear Teaching of God's Word

So they may grow in Christ, walk in truth, preserve the hope of salvation, and help make disciples.
