What to Expect on Sunday

We want you to feel at home from the moment you arrive. Here is what a typical Sunday morning looks like at New Zion.

1
Arrive & Be Welcomed

You will be greeted warmly at the door. There is no dress code, come as you are.

2
Worship & Praise

We open with music and praise that sets the tone for a Spirit-led service.

3
The Message

Scripture-rooted, clearly taught, and applied to real life. Bring a Bible and follow along.

4
Meet & Greet

We take time to welcome one another, in person and online. You will know you are seen.

Getting Here

Parking & Accessibility

Parking

Street parking is available on Call Place SE on both sides of the street, as well as on adjoining side streets.

Several other churches are located nearby. Please be respectful of their designated parking areas.

We recommend arriving a few minutes early on Sunday to allow time to find parking before the 11:15 AM service.

Accessibility

Main entrance: There are three steps from street level to the main entrance.

Side entrance: A step-free side entrance is available and recommended for wheelchair users. Please use this entrance freely, no need to ask.

Inside: The sanctuary and all ministry spaces are on one level, except the pulpit and choir loft.

If you have specific accessibility needs or questions before your visit, please reach out to us.

Optional

Let Us Know You're Coming

You are always welcome to simply show up on Sunday, no advance notice needed. But if you would like us to know you are coming, we would love to hear from you.

Send us a note and we will make sure someone is ready to greet you personally when you arrive.
