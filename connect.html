Prayer & Support

We Are Believing With You

New Zion Baptist Church believes in the power of prayer. Whatever you are facing — a personal need, a family situation, a health concern, or something you cannot yet put into words — we want to stand with you.

Every prayer request is received by our pastoral team personally. If you are facing something urgent, please select that option in the form — your message will be prioritized.

“Do not be anxious about anything, but in every situation, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God.”

Philippians 4:6

If you are in immediate crisis, please go to our Lifeline page or call/text 988.

Submit a Prayer Request

Your request goes directly to our pastoral team. You may remain anonymous if you prefer.

Membership

Interested in Joining New Zion?

We are glad you are considering making New Zion your church home. Membership is not just a formality — it is a commitment to community, to growth, and to the mission God has given this congregation.

Submit your interest using the form and our ministry team will be in touch within one business day.
