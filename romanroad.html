The Gospel

The Roman Road to Salvation

A simple, Scripture-by-Scripture path — using the book of Romans — that answers the most important question anyone can ask: how do I know I'm right with God?

“For I am not ashamed of the gospel, for it is the power of God for salvation to everyone who believes.”
Romans 1:16
What This Means

You Don't Have to Wonder

These six truths, all from the book of Romans, lay out exactly what separates every person from God — and exactly what God has already done to close that gap.

Romans 3:23

We Are Sinners

All have sinned and fall short of the glory of God. No one is exempt — not by effort, not by good intentions.

Romans 6:23

Sin's Penalty

The wages of sin is death. Sin has a real cost, and we cannot pay it ourselves.

Romans 5:8

God's Love Provided

While we were still sinners, Christ died for us. God did not wait for us to get it right first.

Romans 10:9–10

We Must Believe & Confess

If you confess with your mouth and believe in your heart that God raised Jesus from the dead, you will be saved.

Romans 6:23b

We Receive God's Gift

The gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord — a gift, not a wage we could ever earn.

Romans 8:1, 38–39, 10:13

We Have Assurance

There is no condemnation for those in Christ. Nothing can separate us from God's love. Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.

A Simple Prayer

If You're Ready to Take This Step

Salvation isn't about saying the exact right words — it's about a sincere heart turning to God. If this is where you are today, you might pray something like this, in your own words:

Lord Jesus, I know I am a sinner, and I cannot save myself. I believe You died for my sins and rose again. I turn to You now and ask You to save me and make me new. I confess You as my Lord. Thank You for Your gift of eternal life.
