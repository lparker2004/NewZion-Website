Frequently Asked Questions

Quick Answers

The basics, answered simply. If you don't see what you're looking for, reach out and we'll help directly.

What is your address?

New Zion Baptist Church is located at 5400 Call Place SE, Washington, D.C.

What is the office email?

You can reach the church office anytime at info@newzionbaptist.org. We respond within 1–2 business days.

How do I get directions?

We're at 5400 Call Place SE, Washington, D.C. Street parking is available on both sides of Call Place SE and on adjoining side streets. A step-free side entrance is available if you need it, no need to ask, just use it freely.

Visit our Visit Us page for a map and full details on what to expect.

How do I invite a friend?

However feels natural. Invite them to Sunday worship at 11:15 AM, in person or by sending them our live stream link. Or invite them to Wednesday Bible Study, a low-pressure way for someone to see what New Zion is really like.

You can also simply share our website, newzionbaptist.org, it has everything they'd want to know before ever walking through the door.

How do I share my faith with someone?

Start with your own story, what God has done in your life is something no one can argue with. When the conversation opens up to the gospel itself, our Roman Road to Salvation page walks through it clearly, Scripture by Scripture, it's a great thing to send someone or read through together.

And if you're ever unsure what to say, you're never alone in this, reach out to Pastor Parker directly and he's glad to help you think it through.

What are your service times?

Sunday Worship: 11:15 AM, in person and online.
Wednesday Bible Study: 7:00–8:00 PM, online via Zoom.

Is it better to worship online or in person?

Both are wonderful ways to worship with us. We simply want to see you regularly, whichever fits your season of life. Online worshippers are treated as real members of the congregation, not just viewers, greeted by name in the chat and prayed for just the same.

What does the Bible say about giving?

Scripture calls believers to give generously, cheerfully, and consistently, as an act of worship, not an obligation. "Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver" (2 Corinthians 9:7). Many in our congregation follow the biblical pattern of tithing, giving the first portion back to God, though giving at New Zion is always a response to grace, never a requirement for belonging.

Learn more on our Give page.

How do I connect to Givelify?

Visit giv.li/5e28vt or download the free Givelify app and search for New Zion Baptist Church. You can set up a one-time gift or recurring giving in just a minute or two, securely.
